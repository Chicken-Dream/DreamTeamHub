Signing in is optional

Every game on dreamteamhub.ca can be played as a guest. Signing in with Google only matters for leaderboards.

What we store when you sign in

  • A Google account ID. Google gives us a random number that identifies your account to us. We don't ask for, receive or store your email address, real name or photo.
  • Your display name, like "LuckyOtter". It starts random and you can change it from the menu at the top of the home page. Other players can see it.
  • A login cookie called dth_session, set on dreamteamhub.ca so every game knows you're signed in. It lasts 60 days or until you sign out. We only keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • Game results (wins, rankings and similar) once leaderboards are added, linked to your account.

We don't sell or share this information. It's stored with Cloudflare, which runs our account service.

Ads and other services

The home page shows ads from Google AdSense, which may use its own cookies. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. The "Support us" button links to Ko-fi, which has its own privacy policy.

Delete your account

This permanently deletes your account, display name, results and every login session. You can sign in again later, but you'll start over as a new player.
